Privacy policy

Your notes never leave your disk.

Most privacy policies explain what a company does with the data it collects about you. This one is shorter, because we collect none. BobNote has no servers, no accounts, and no analytics. Your notes are files in a folder you chose, on a disk you own.

Effective August 26, 2026

Your notes stay on your machine

Everything you put into BobNote — notes, files, pictures, links, drawings — is written into a BobFolder on your own disk. We never receive it, store it, scan it, index it, or train anything on it. We could not hand it over to anyone, because we never have it.

We collect nothing

No accounts. No sign-in. No analytics, no usage statistics, no crash reporting that phones home, no advertising identifiers, no trackers, no telemetry of any kind. BobNote does not need the network to do its job, and it does not use it to report on you.

This website is static

The site you are reading is a handful of plain files. It runs no scripts, sets no cookies, embeds no fonts or images from other companies, and loads nothing from any third-party server. There is no form here, so there is nothing here to submit.

To be exact about one thing: the company that hosts these files keeps ordinary server logs, as every web host does, and those logs can include the IP address that requested a page. We do not receive those logs, we have no analytics layered on top of them, and nothing about your visit reaches us.

Payments are handled by Apple

Subscriptions are sold through the App Store. Apple handles the purchase, the payment details, and the renewal, under Apple's own privacy policy. We are told whether a subscription is active — never who you are or how you paid.

Third parties

Apple, for the purchase above, is the only third party involved in BobNote. We use no analytics vendor, no error-tracking vendor, no advertising network, and no data broker. We have no data to sell, and we would not sell it if we did.

If you choose to keep your BobFolder inside a cloud-sync folder, that is your decision and your provider's terms apply to those files — the choice, and the control, stay with you.

Your rights, and why there is nothing to request

Privacy law gives you the right to see, correct, export, and delete the personal data a company holds about you. We hold none, so there is nothing for us to show you or erase. Your data is already in your hands: open the folder, read the files, copy them, or delete them. No request, no waiting period, and no permission needed from us.

Children

BobNote is not directed at children, and since it collects no data at all, it collects no data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. The commitment above — no servers, no collection — is the product, not a setting, so any change to it would be announced plainly rather than buried in a revision.

Questions

Write to support@bobnote.sg and a human will answer.
