Frequently asked

The essentials

What is a BobFolder?

A portable BobFolder keeps a Board, its nested Boards and included files together. Use Create BobFolder to save an independent copy, then transfer the complete folder with a cloud drive, USB drive or another method you trust. On the destination device, Combine a BobFolder adds it to your current Board without replacing your existing work. This is a deliberate transfer, not automatic synchronization.

Where are my files?

Your working library stays in BobNote’s local storage on your device. Open Settings → BobFolder Storage to see its size and full path. Use Create BobFolder when you want a portable copy in a location of your choice. BobNote does not upload your notes to a BobNote server.

How do I back up my notes?

Keep device backups and independent copies of important projects. With Premium, open the project Board and use Create BobFolder, then keep the complete exported folder on another drive or in your chosen backup service. Start from Home to include its Board tree. The portable copy contains that exported project; it is not an exact backup of every app setting, undo step or item in the Basket.

How can I keep my work useful outside BobNote?

Export a Board to PDF for reading and sharing. A portable BobFolder also keeps included files alongside BobNote’s Board data. Those files can be opened with compatible apps; the editable canvas and its relationships are read by BobNote. Keep a separate copy before clearing local storage or removing the app.

What does Premium add?

Four things: the mindmap card and mind-map view, creating a BobFolder from a board, combining boards from separate BobFolders, and PDF export without the small BobNote mark in the corner. Everything else is in the app from the first launch, and Premium is a one-time unlock rather than a subscription. Mac and iPad share your App Store purchases when you use the same Apple Account: buy BobNote once for both, and a Premium unlock works on both too. The Windows edition and its Premium upgrade are purchased separately through the Microsoft Store. Apple and Microsoft purchases are not interchangeable.

Why is there a mark on my PDF?

PDF export works from the start, and until you unlock Premium the exported page carries a small BobNote mark in the corner. It sits on the rendered PDF only — your notes and the files in your BobFolder are never marked or altered, and the same board exports clean once Premium is unlocked.

Something went wrong with a purchase — who do I ask?

The store you bought from. On Mac and iPad that is Apple, and receipts and refunds are managed in your App Store account settings. On Windows it is Microsoft, and they are managed in your Microsoft account's order history.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

Not for BobNote itself. There is no BobNote account to create, and BobNote works with the network switched off. Buying and installing it uses your App Store or Microsoft Store account, and a connection is needed the way any app from a store needs one: to install it and its updates, make a purchase, restore it or verify a license. Download the files you need before going offline. Playing a YouTube video, or an audio or video link on a card, needs a connection too.

Found a bug, or want a feature?

Tell us what you were doing, what you expected and what happened instead. For an error, open Settings → Diagnostics → Save Error Report to PDF. Review the saved report, then send it with a short description through Contact Support. Reports are not sent automatically. Use email or a direct message for reports containing personal details, rather than the public Telegram group. Feature suggestions are welcome too.
