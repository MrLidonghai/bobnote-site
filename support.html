Frequently asked

The essentials

What is a BobFolder?

A BobFolder is an ordinary folder on your disk that holds one BobNote library: the boards you build and every file you drop into them, kept together in one place. BobNote keeps it in a fixed place on your own computer — on Windows, the BobNote folder in your Documents — and you can copy it and back it up the same as any other folder. On iPad the library lives inside the app, and a BobFolder is how boards travel in and out through the Files app.

Where are my files?

On a Mac or a PC, inside the BobFolder on your own disk. On a Mac, select a file card and choose Show to see that file inside your BobFolder in Finder. On Windows, type %USERPROFILE%\Documents\BobNote into the File Explorer address bar (usually C:\Users\your user folder\Documents\BobNote) to see your material sitting there. On iPad, inside the app's own storage, which your iPad backup includes. Nothing is uploaded, because there is no server to upload to.

How do I back up my notes?

Copy the folder. That is the whole procedure — to an external drive, to another machine, into whatever backup system you already trust. The copy is a real backup, readable without asking us for anything.

Will I ever lose access to my own notes?

No. They stay exactly where they are, on your disk, in plain files you can open with something else. What you unlock changes what the app can do; it never changes what you can reach. Nothing is locked, held, or encrypted against you.

What does Premium add?

Four things: the mindmap card and mind-map view, creating a BobFolder from a board, combining boards from separate BobFolders, and PDF export without the small BobNote mark in the corner. Everything else is in the app from the first launch, and Premium is a one-time unlock rather than a subscription. There is nothing to renew and nothing that lapses. Mac and iPad share one App Store purchase, so Premium unlocked on one is unlocked on the other. The Windows edition is sold separately through the Microsoft Store; Premium there is coming in a later update.

Why is there a mark on my PDF?

PDF export works from the start, and until you unlock Premium the exported page carries a small BobNote mark in the corner. It sits on the rendered PDF only — your notes and the files in your BobFolder are never marked or altered, and the same board exports clean once Premium is unlocked.

Something went wrong with a purchase — who do I ask?

The store you bought from. On Mac and iPad that is Apple, and receipts and refunds are managed in your App Store account settings. On Windows it is Microsoft, and they are managed in your Microsoft account's order history.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

Not for BobNote itself. There is no BobNote account to create, and BobNote works with the network switched off. Buying and installing it uses your App Store or Microsoft Store account, and a connection is needed the way any app from a store needs one: to install it and its updates. Playing a YouTube video, or an audio or video link on a card, needs a connection too.

Found a bug, or want a feature?

Write to support@bobnote.app. Tell us what you expected and what happened instead — that is usually enough to reproduce it. Feature requests are read, and the good ones get built.
