See your project. Not just its filenames.

Put photos, documents, links and notes side by side in a Board. Group related material and keep the details in nested Boards. Your project takes shape as a visual workspace, with the source files close at hand.

Turn information into a point of view.

Compare alternatives, mark important details and map out your thinking. Keep your conclusions beside the material that informed them. Use mindmaps to explore, tables to compare, and to-do lists to turn decisions into next steps.

The place you think becomes the place you present.

Walk others through your canvas in a meeting or a screen share. Gather the key findings into a focused Board, or export a PDF to send afterwards. Keep the supporting material in the same project, ready when a question takes you deeper.

Your work stays with you.

Your working library stays on your device. Saved notes and local files work offline. No BobNote account is needed.

Take the project. Keep the context.

Create a BobFolder from a selected Board, with its nested Boards and included files. Transfer the complete folder by USB, Wi-Fi file transfer or your chosen cloud drive. The recipient uses Combine a BobFolder to import it. This is a handoff between copies, not live sync or automatic merging.
