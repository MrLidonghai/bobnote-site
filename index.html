A project starts with a collection: photos, documents, links and notes from different places. Bring that material into a Project Board, where you can lay out what matters and see it in context.

Boards nest like folders. Inside each one, arrange visible content, add explanations and keep related material side by side.

Your source material, working thoughts and finished outcomes stay in the same project.

Choose what travels

Share a project.
Or just the part that matters.

Keep your working material together, then choose a project or presentation Board to send. You do not have to share your whole workspace.

  1. 1

    Create a BobFolder

    Export the Board you have open, its nested Boards and included files as a portable copy.

  2. 2

    Use your own route

    Transfer the complete folder by USB, Wi-Fi file transfer or a cloud drive such as Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox.

  3. 3

    Combine and continue

    Import it into BobNote on another device. A colleague can work on their copy and send an updated BobFolder back for you to review and bring into your workspace.

See Create and Combine in action →

Create and Combine require Premium. BobFolders are independent copies; exchanging them is not live synchronization or automatic merging.

Worth knowing before you buy

Four things need Premium.

Every card type but one, the whole canvas, nesting, connections, linked copies, the recycle area, and your BobFolder on your own disk — all of it is there when you open the app. Premium is a one-time unlock, not a subscription, and it adds these four.

  • Mindmap

    The mindmap card, and the mind-map view of a board.

  • Create a BobFolder from a board

    Promote any board into a BobFolder of its own.

  • Combine BobFolder boards

    Bring boards from separate BobFolders together.

  • PDF export without the mark

    You can export and print to PDF from the start. Until Premium, the page carries a small BobNote mark in the corner. Your notes themselves are never marked, locked, or altered — the mark is on the rendered PDF only.
