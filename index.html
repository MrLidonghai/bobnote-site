What BobNote is

See the relationships without giving up ownership.

01

One infinite canvas

Lay everything out and look at it at once: matrix, mind map, and board views of the same material. Draw connections between cards, and let a linked copy stay in sync across boards. Boards nest inside boards — double-click one to step inside.

02

Files are the asset

Keep images, video, PDFs, documents and notes together with the context that makes them useful. A portable BobFolder carries the project and its included files. Your local material stays available when the network is not.

03

Built for bad days

Deleted cards wait in the Basket, and Archive checks and seals a local checkpoint. Clear feedback helps you spot save or transfer problems. Keep independent copies of important projects too — a checkpoint is not a complete backup.

04

Ready for your AI tools

Choose which exported files to share with your preferred AI tools. Use a separate copy, review the results, and bring back only what helps. BobNote does not automatically send your project to an AI service.

How it works

Apps come and go. Your BobFolder is the record.

  1. 1

    Work on the canvas

    Drop in notes, files, pictures, and links. Arrange them until the shape of the thing is obvious at a glance.

  2. 2

    It saves on your device

    Work in BobNote’s local library. Your notes are not uploaded to a BobNote server, and local editing does not depend on a cloud connection.

  3. 3

    Take the folder anywhere

    Create a BobFolder, transfer the whole folder with your chosen cloud drive or USB drive, then Combine it on another device. Share a PDF when your audience only needs to view it.

Worth knowing before you buy

Four things need Premium.

Every card type but one, the whole canvas, nesting, connections, linked copies, the recycle area, and your BobFolder on your own disk — all of it is there when you open the app. Premium is a one-time unlock, not a subscription, and it adds these four.

  • Mindmap

    The mindmap card, and the mind-map view of a board.

  • Create a BobFolder from a board

    Promote any board into a BobFolder of its own.

  • Combine BobFolder boards

    Bring boards from separate BobFolders together.

  • PDF export without the mark

    You can export and print to PDF from the start. Until Premium, the page carries a small BobNote mark in the corner. Your notes themselves are never marked, locked, or altered — the mark is on the rendered PDF only.
